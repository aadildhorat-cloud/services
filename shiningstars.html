About Us

Dedicated to serving our community with compassion and integrity

Making a Difference Since 2019

Shining Stars Wellness and Recovery is a registered Non-Profit Organization dedicated to uplifting communities through various charitable initiatives. We are not funded by any institute or government – our work is purely supported by generous public donors and sponsors who believe in our mission.

From grocery hampers to educational support, from feeding schemes to medical assistance, we strive to be a beacon of hope for those in need. Our continuous bread and milk project, ongoing since COVID-19, demonstrates our commitment to sustained community support.
